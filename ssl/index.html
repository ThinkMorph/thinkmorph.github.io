    Motivation

    Images can supply their own supervision.

    Models that reason with intermediate images are usually trained on traces built for one task family at a time. Self-supervised pretext tasks need no such traces, because the original image is the answer. ThinkMorph-SSL turns these tasks into interleaved reasoning traces and trains on them before instruction tuning. Compared with the same model reasoning in text, it gains 9.84 points on perception, 4.22 on static spatial understanding and 17.92 on mental world modeling.

    Method

    Pretext reasoning comes first, and instruction tuning follows.

    Results

    We evaluate on twelve benchmarks in three levels. ThinkMorph-SSL beats the same model reasoning in text on all twelve, with the largest margin on mental world modeling.

    Analysis

    Pretext reasoning raises accuracy, and it works best as its own stage before instruction tuning.

    Order and instructions

    Pretext training before instruction tuning reaches 66.6% on MindCube, while mixing both corpora stays near 57%. Instruction-free pretext traces lead instructed ones by 2.7 points on average.

    Cost

    TeaCache cuts the latency per V⋆ question from 62.3 s to 21.3 s without lowering accuracy.
